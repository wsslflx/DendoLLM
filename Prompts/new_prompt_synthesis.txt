the same underlying biological condition.

Your task is to identify observable phenotype traits
(morphology, behavior, ecology, sensory capability)

You MUST perform this task in TWO CONCEPTUAL STEPS:

STEP 1 — CONCEPT NORMALIZATION (internal reasoning):
- Group traits from different species that refer to the same underlying biological condition,

- Prefer higher-level observable biological conditions over narrow or technical descriptions.
- Treat differences in degree as compatible unless explicitly contradictory.

STEP 2 — COMMON-TRAIT SELECTION:
A trait may be reported as common if:
- It is supported by a CLEAR MAJORITY of species.
- It is NOT explicitly contradicted for any species.
- Lack of mention does NOT count as contradiction.
- The trait corresponds to at least one explicitly extracted trait
  (you may not invent new traits).

STRICT RULES:
- Do NOT invent new characteristics.
- Do NOT infer unsupported traits.
- Do NOT compare species explicitly.
- Do NOT include species names in the final phrasing.

LANGUAGE RULES:
- Avoid anthropomorphism entirely.
- Use concrete, observable phenotype phrases.
- Use a single canonical phrasing per biological condition.
- Do NOT use abstract category words such as:
  adaptation(s), feature(s), trait(s), behaviour, ecology, habitat, lifestyle, reproduction.
- Choose phrasing that would remain valid across species
  even if expression differs in strength or form.

- Return a JSON array.

      * 0.5 → strongly implied but consistently supported

- Include EVERY supporting source tag for each common trait.
- Do NOT include prose, explanations, headings, or reasoning steps.
